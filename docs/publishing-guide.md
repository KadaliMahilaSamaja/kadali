# Publishing guide — Kadali website

This guide is for Kadali club members.
It explains how to write an article, upload photos, create an event, and send your work for publishing.
You only need your phone. You do not need to know anything about websites.

Other documents: the [admin guide](admin-guide.md) is for the club administrator. The [architecture overview](architecture.md) explains how the website works. The [validation report](validation.md) is the research this system is based on.

---

## What you need

1. An Android phone with the Chrome browser (update Chrome if it is old).
2. A free GitHub account. Create one at github.com if you do not have one. Your account name is like `smitraju`.
3. The administrator must add you to the club once. Ask her for your GitHub account name.

## Important things to know

- The editing screen is in English. You can type Kannada inside every text box. Use the Kannada keyboard on your phone (Gboard: ಕನ್ನಡ).
- Nothing you save is shown on the website immediately. The administrator checks it and then publishes it (ಪ್ರಕಟಿಸುವುದು).
- Do not click **Publish**. Publishing is the administrator's job. A rule on the website also stops a writer from publishing her own work alone.

## 1. Sign in

```
+----------------------------------------+
| 1. Open Chrome                          |
| 2. Go to  kadali.example.org/admin      |
| 3. Click [ Sign in with GitHub ]        |
| 4. Enter your GitHub name and password  |
+----------------------------------------+
```

1. Open Chrome on your phone.
2. Type the website address and add `/admin` at the end, for example `kadali.example.org/admin`.
3. Choose the GitHub sign-in button (**Sign in with GitHub**) [VERIFY: exact button wording on the login screen].
4. GitHub asks for your account name and password. Enter them.
5. The first time only, GitHub asks you to allow the Kadali app. Click **Authorize** (ಅನುಮತಿಸಿ).
6. You now see the editing screen. The list on the left shows: **Articles**, **Events**, **Announcements**, **Gallery**, **Homepage**.

Tip: in Chrome, open the menu and choose **Add to Home screen**. Then the editor opens like an app.

## 2. Write an article

```
+------------------------------------------+
| + button > Articles > fill the boxes      |
| > Save > Mark as Ready                    |
+------------------------------------------+
```

1. Tap the **+** button (Quick Add) at the top and choose **Articles**.
2. A blank article form opens. Fill in the boxes:

   - **Title** (ಶೀರ್ಷಿಕೆ) — the name of your article. You can write it in Kannada or English.
   - **Description** (ಸಂಕ್ಷಿಪ್ತ ವಿವರ) — one or two sentences about the article. Keep it short, about 200 letters.
   - **Cover image** — the main photo. See section 3 below.
   - **Cover image description** — one line saying what the photo shows.
   - **Author** (ಲೇಖಕಿ) — your name.
   - **Category** — choose one: culture, literature, seva, spirituality, community.
   - **Publish date** — the date shown with the article. The today date is filled in for you.
   - **Featured on homepage** — leave it off. The administrator chooses this.
   - **Draft** — leave it off.
   - **Body** (ಮುಖ್ಯ ಭಾಗ) — write your article here. Use the toolbar for **B** bold, headings, lists, and links.

3. Tap **Save** (ಉಳಿಸಿ) at the top. Your work is now safe, even if the phone switches off.
4. A question appears: **Mark as Ready** or **Later**.

   - Tap **Mark as Ready** (ಸಿದ್ಧ) when your article is complete. This tells the administrator: "please check and publish".
   - Tap **Later** (ಆಮೇಲೆ) if you want to continue another day. Your article stays as a draft.

5. Close the page. You can open your draft again any time under **Articles**.

Your article is NOT on the website yet.
The administrator reads it, then publishes it. A few minutes later it appears on the Articles page.

## 3. Upload a photo

Photos can come from your phone gallery or directly from the camera.

```
+--------------------------------------------+
| Tap the picture box                         |
| > [ Upload ] > Gallery or Camera            |
| > choose photo > done                       |
+--------------------------------------------+
```

1. Tap the picture box, for example **Cover image** or **Poster**.
2. Choose the upload option (**Upload**).
3. Your phone opens the gallery or camera. Choose or take a photo.
4. Wait a few seconds. The photo appears in the box.

Good to know:

- A photo can be at most **15 MB** (15 ಮೆಗಾಬೈಟ್). Normal phone photos are 2 to 8 MB, so they are fine.
- The system makes big photos smaller by itself (maximum 2048 pixels, WebP format). You do not need to resize anything.
- The photo is saved inside the club's project. Nothing is lost.

### Photo album (Gallery)

1. Tap the **+** button and choose **Gallery**.
2. Fill in **Title**, **Date**, and optionally **Event**.
3. Under **Photos**, use the add-photo button (**Add Photo**) [VERIFY: exact button wording] to add one row per photo.
4. In each row, tap **Image** to choose a photo, and type an optional **Caption** (ಶೀರ್ಷಿಕೆ ಕೆಳಗೆ ಬರೆಯುವ ಸಣ್ಣ ಟಿಪ್ಪಣಿ).
5. Tap **Save**, then **Mark as Ready**.

## 4. Create an event

```
+------------------------------------------+
| + button > Events > fill the boxes        |
| > Save > Mark as Ready                    |
+------------------------------------------+
```

1. Tap the **+** button and choose **Events**.
2. Fill in the boxes:

   - **Title** (ಕಾರ್ಯಕ್ರಮದ ಹೆಸರು) — for example "Republic Day Satsang".
   - **Poster** — the event poster photo.
   - **Summary** — one or two sentences about the event.
   - **Starts at** (ಪ್ರಾರಂಭ) — date and time the event starts.
   - **Ends at** — optional. Leave empty if you do not know.
   - **Venue** (ಸ್ಥಳ) — the place, for example "Kadali Bhavi, Sirsi".
   - **Map link** — optional. Paste the Google Maps link of the place.
   - **Contact** — optional. A name or phone number for questions.
   - **Status** — keep **auto**. The website shows "upcoming" before the date and "completed" after, by itself.
   - **Featured on homepage** — leave off. The administrator chooses.
   - **Details** — more information, if any.

3. Tap **Save**, then **Mark as Ready**.

The event appears on the website after the administrator publishes it.

---

## 5. What happens after you send your work

1. Your saved work sits in a safe waiting place. It is not shown on the website.
2. The administrator opens it, reads it, and clicks **Publish** (ಪ್ರಕಟಿಸಿ).
3. The website rebuilds itself. After one or two minutes your article or event is live.
4. You can check it: open the website and look.

If you want to change something later, open your piece under its collection, edit it, and tap **Save** again. The administrator checks the change the same way.

---

## 6. Simple rules

- Do. Save often. Your typing is kept even if you close the page.
- Do. Write in Kannada, English, or both.
- Do. Ask the administrator when something looks wrong.
- Do not. Click **Publish**. The administrator does that.
- Do not. Edit anything on the github.com website directly.
- Do not. Share your GitHub password with anyone.

## 7. Problems and answers

| Problem | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The sign-in page does not accept me | You have not been added to the club yet | Send your GitHub account name to the administrator |
| Photo is rejected | Bigger than 15 MB | Take the photo again at a smaller size, or ask the administrator |
| I clicked **Publish** by mistake and it failed | Writers cannot publish their own work alone. This is by design | Nothing is broken. Ask the administrator to publish |
| "Update your browser" message | Chrome is too old | Update Chrome in the Play Store |
| I cannot find my saved article | You are looking in the wrong collection | Check under **Articles**, **Events**, or **Gallery** in the left list |
| Kannada letters look strange | Font issue on the phone | The website uses a Kannada font; if it still looks wrong, tell the administrator |

## 9. Update the committee

The committee members (ಸಮಿತಿ) shown on the About page are managed in the CMS too.

1. In the sidebar, tap **Committee**, then **New Member**.
2. Fill in her **Name** (and her name in Kannada), choose her **Role**, add her **Photo**, and write a short **Short introduction**.
3. Tap **Save**, then submit it for review.
4. After the administrator publishes, she appears on the About page in her role group.

You only choose the role in English in the form — the website shows the Kannada role name (ಅಧ್ಯಕ್ಷೆ, ಕಾರ್ಯದರ್ಶಿ, ಕೋಶಾಧಿಕಾರಿ, ಸದಸ್ಯೆ …) by itself.
